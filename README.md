# Statistical Arbitrage in Indian Equity Markets

## Overview

This repository contains research and implementation work on **Statistical Arbitrage in Indian Equity Markets**, conducted as part of an academic research internship.

The primary objective of this project is to investigate data-driven approaches for identifying and exploiting temporary mispricing opportunities among Indian equities. The study focuses on constructing market-neutral trading strategies using historical stock data, firm-specific characteristics, latent factor models, and modern machine learning techniques.

The project aims to adapt and evaluate methodologies proposed in contemporary statistical arbitrage literature within the context of the Indian stock market.

---

## Motivation

Financial markets exhibit complex cross-sectional and temporal dependencies that are often not fully captured by traditional valuation approaches. Statistical arbitrage seeks to identify systematic patterns in asset returns and exploit deviations from their expected behavior.

Recent advances in machine learning and factor modeling have enabled the development of more sophisticated representations of market dynamics, offering new opportunities for constructing profitable and robust trading strategies.

This project explores whether such approaches can be effectively applied to Indian equities and how efficient such approaches are.

---

## Objectives

* Collect and process large-scale historical equity data from Indian stock markets.
* Construct and analyze firm-level characteristics and return-based features.
* Study latent factor structures underlying equity returns.
* Develop market-neutral statistical arbitrage strategies.
* Investigate machine learning approaches for factor discovery and portfolio construction.
* Evaluate strategy performance using realistic backtesting frameworks.
* Analyze risk-adjusted returns, drawdowns, turnover, and transaction-cost sensitivity.
* Compare alternative factor modeling and trading methodologies.

---

## Scope

The project focuses on a universe of highly liquid Indian equities and uses historical market data spanning multiple years.

## Research Questions

Some of the key questions investigated in this project include:

* Can latent factor models improve statistical arbitrage performance in Indian markets?
* Which firm characteristics provide the most useful information for explaining cross-sectional returns?
* Can machine learning methods identify trading opportunities efficiently?
* How robust are statistical arbitrage strategies under realistic trading assumptions?
